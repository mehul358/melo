# Product

## Audience

The site is a personal family hub. Its current experiences are designed primarily
for children learning Hindi and learning about Portugal.

## Product principles

- Make every experience usable without an account when practical.
- Favor clear, playful, touch-friendly interfaces.
- Keep pages fast and dependable on mobile devices.
- Use audio, maps, games, and shared state only when they add clear value.
- Protect family data and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
- Keep the deployment and maintenance burden low.

## Current experiences

### Hindi

Interactive lessons, stories, quizzes, packing and observation games, and Hindi/
English speech playback. Progress is local to the browser.

### Portugal for kids

A playful guide to places, language, food, animals, history, packing, songs, quizzes,
and memory games, with weather and speech features.

## Out of scope by default

Do not add authentication, a new backend, a database abstraction, analytics, or a
JavaScript build framework unless a concrete feature requires it.
